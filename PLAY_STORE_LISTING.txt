FULL DESCRIPTION  (4000 characters max)
==========================================================================

Ever picked up a beautiful stone and wondered what it actually is? Crystal
Genie tells you in seconds. Point your camera at any crystal, take a photo,
and our AI identifies it instantly — then tells you everything worth knowing
about it.

IDENTIFY ANY CRYSTAL FROM A PHOTO

Snap a new photo or choose one from your gallery. Crystal Genie's
image-recognition model analyses the stone's colour, form, and surface and
returns the most likely match along with a confidence score, so you always
know how certain the result is.

Works on raw specimens, polished tumbles, clusters, points, spheres, and
jewellery pieces.

LEARN THE STORY BEHIND EVERY STONE

Each identification opens a full profile of the crystal:

• What it is and where it comes from
• Its traditional meaning and symbolism
• The chakras it is traditionally associated with
• The zodiac and star signs it is linked to in crystal lore
• How collectors typically care for and cleanse it

BUILD YOUR COLLECTION

Save any crystal you identify to your personal collection. Your saved stones
sync to your account, so your collection follows you to any device you sign
in on. Browse back through everything you have discovered, any time.

EXPLORE AND DISCOVER

Not sure what you are looking for? Browse the full crystal library to explore
stones by name, meaning, and association — a genuine reference for anyone
building knowledge alongside a collection.

SHOP AUTHENTIC CRYSTALS

Found a stone you love? Order it. Crystal Genie includes a built-in shop of
hand-picked crystals and specimens, with a simple cart, secure card checkout
powered by Stripe, and an emailed order confirmation with every purchase.
Save your shipping address once and reorder in a couple of taps.

WHY CRYSTAL GENIE

• Instant results — identification takes seconds, not minutes
• Confidence scores, so you can judge every result for yourself
• No ads, anywhere in the app
• Your photos are never stored — images are analysed and immediately
  discarded
• Sign in with email, Google, or Apple
• Clean, calm interface designed to stay out of your way

FREE TRIAL, THEN A SIMPLE SUBSCRIPTION

Start with a free trial to try crystal identification with no commitment.
After the trial, Crystal Genie Premium unlocks unlimited scanning for
USD 3.69 per month. Your subscription renews automatically and can be
cancelled any time from inside the app or from your Google Play account
settings; cancelling stops future charges and you keep access until the end
of the period you have paid for.

Browsing the crystal library and shopping the store are always free.

COMMON VISUAL ASSETS — all required before you can publish:

  App icon         512 x 512 px, 32-bit PNG, max 1 MB, no transparency,
                   no rounded corners (Play adds them)
  Feature graphic  1024 x 500 px, PNG or JPEG, max 15 MB, no transparency.
                   Keep text away from the edges — it gets cropped on some
                   surfaces
  Phone screenshots  Minimum 2, maximum 8. Recommend 4-6.
                   16:9 or 9:16 aspect ratio, each side 320-3840 px.
                   1080 x 1920 px is the safe choice.
  Tablet screenshots  Optional, but without them your app is excluded from
                   some tablet and Chromebook surfaces

Suggested screenshot order (this order matters — most people only see the
first two):
  1. Camera aimed at a crystal, mid-scan
  2. Result screen showing the identified crystal and its confidence score
  3. Crystal detail page with meaning, chakras, star sign
  4. Saved collection screen
  5. Shop screen
  6. Paywall / trial screen

Add a short caption banner to each screenshot image ("Scan any crystal",
"Know exactly what you are holding") — plain screenshots convert noticeably
worse than captioned ones.

VIDEO — optional, must be a YouTube URL, not an upload. Skip it for launch.
