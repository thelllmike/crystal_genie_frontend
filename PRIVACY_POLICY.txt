Last updated: 13 August 2026

Crystal Genie ("the App", "we", "us", "our") is a mobile application that
identifies crystals from photographs and sells crystals and related products.
This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it,
who we share it with, and the choices you have.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.1 Account information

When you sign up with an email address and password, we collect:

  - Your name
  - Your email address
  - Your password (stored only as a salted hash by our authentication
    provider; we never see or store your password in readable form)

If you choose to sign in with Google or Apple instead, we receive your name
and email address from that provider. We do not receive your Google or Apple
password.

1.2 Photographs and camera access

The core feature of the App is crystal identification. To use it you may
take a photo with your device camera or select an existing photo from your
device gallery. The App requests camera and photo library permission only
for this purpose, and only at the moment you use the feature.

The selected image is uploaded over an encrypted HTTPS connection to our
server, where a machine-learning model analyses it and returns the likely
crystal name and related information.

We do not store your photographs. Images are held in server memory only for
the seconds needed to run the analysis and are then discarded. They are not
saved to disk, not added to any database, not used to train our model, and
not shared with anyone.

What we do keep is an anonymous record of each identification result: the
name of the crystal detected and the model's confidence score. This record
is not linked to your account, your device, or your image, and is used only
to understand which crystals are commonly scanned.

1.3 Order and shipping information

If you buy a product through the App, you provide:

  - Recipient name
  - Phone number
  - Street address, city, and postal code

We use this solely to process, package, and deliver your order and to
respond to questions about it. If you tick the option to remember your
address, it is saved to your account so that you do not have to type it
again; you may change or clear it at any time from the checkout screen.

1.4 Payment information

Payments are processed by Stripe, Inc. Card details are entered into
Stripe's own secure payment sheet and are sent directly to Stripe. We never
receive, see, or store your full card number, expiry date, or security code.

From Stripe we receive only what we need to fulfil your order and manage
your subscription: whether the payment succeeded, the amount, the currency,
the last four digits and card brand, and a Stripe customer or subscription
identifier.

1.5 Subscription information

The App offers a free trial followed by an optional paid subscription
(currently USD 3.69 per month) which unlocks crystal scanning. We store your
subscription status, trial end date, current billing period end date, and
whether you have requested cancellation. This status is reported to us by
Stripe.

1.6 App activity

We store items you add to your cart, your saved or favourited crystals, and
your order history, so that these are available to you across sessions and
devices.

1.7 Information we do NOT collect

  - We do not collect your precise or approximate location.
  - We do not collect your contacts, calendar, SMS, or call logs.
  - We do not use advertising identifiers and we do not serve ads.
  - We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  - We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  - We do not knowingly collect information from children (see section 8).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

We use the information described above only to:

  - Create and secure your account and sign you in
  - Identify crystals from the photos you submit
  - Take payment and manage your free trial and subscription
  - Process, ship, and support your orders
  - Send you an order confirmation email after a purchase
  - Send essential service messages, such as email address confirmation and
    password reset
  - Keep the App working correctly and diagnose technical problems
  - Comply with tax, accounting, and other legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with third
parties for their own marketing or advertising.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. SERVICE PROVIDERS WE SHARE DATA WITH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

We use a small number of processors, each of which receives only the data
needed for its function:

  Supabase (authentication and database)
  Stores your account record, cart, saved crystals, orders, and shipping
  address. Privacy policy: https://supabase.com/privacy

  Stripe (payments and subscriptions)
  Processes card payments and recurring billing. Receives your payment
  details directly and your email address for receipts.
  Privacy policy: https://stripe.com/privacy

  Google Sign-In / Apple Sign In (optional login)
  Used only if you choose these sign-in methods. They confirm your identity
  to us and provide your name and email address.
  https://policies.google.com/privacy
  https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

  Email delivery
  Order confirmation and account emails are sent from our own mail server
  (admin@crystalgenie.com) over an encrypted SMTP connection.

We may also disclose information where we are legally required to do so, for
example in response to a valid legal request, or where necessary to protect
our rights, safety, or property, or to prevent fraud.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. DATA SECURITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

All communication between the App and our servers uses HTTPS (TLS)
encryption. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Access to your
account data is protected by row-level security rules, so one user cannot
read another user's data. Card data is handled entirely by Stripe, a
PCI-DSS Level 1 certified provider.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. While we take
reasonable measures to protect your information, we cannot guarantee
absolute security.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. HOW LONG WE KEEP YOUR DATA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Photographs: not retained. Discarded immediately after analysis.
  - Anonymous detection records: retained indefinitely; contain no personal
    data.
  - Account data (name, email, saved crystals, cart, shipping address):
    retained while your account is open, and deleted when you delete your
    account.
  - Order and payment records: retained for as long as required by tax and
    accounting law (typically up to 7 years), even after account deletion.

6. YOUR RIGHTS AND CHOICES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct,
export, restrict the processing of, or delete your personal information, and
to withdraw consent or object to certain processing. To exercise any of
these rights, email admin@crystalgenie.com and we will respond within 30
days.

Account deletion
You may delete your account and its associated personal data at any time by
emailing admin@crystalgenie.com from your registered email address, or by
using the delete-account option in the App's profile screen where available.
Deletion removes your name, email, saved crystals, cart, and stored shipping
address. Order and payment records are retained where the law requires, as
described in section 5.

Permissions
You can revoke camera and photo library access at any time in your device
settings. Crystal identification will not work without it, but the rest of
the App will continue to function.

Marketing
We do not send promotional email. Transactional email (order confirmations,
password resets) is a necessary part of the service.
